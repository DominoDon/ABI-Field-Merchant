## abibuilder.com
- `data.js` is generated by `tools/gen-data.js` from the public merchant page at
  <https://abibuilder.com/merchant> (crate types, loot groups, weights, item names and values).
- Item sounds (`.ogg`) are **not included** in this repository. The app downloads them from
  abibuilder.com at runtime and caches them on the user's PC.
